The Brewers

The club began as the Seattle Pilots in 1969 and moved to Milwaukee for the 1970 season. In 1982 "Harvey's Wallbangers" led the majors in home runs and reached the World Series, the "Suds Series," losing to St. Louis in seven games. That team had five future Hall of Famers: Robin Yount, Paul Molitor, Rollie Fingers, Ted Simmons and Don Sutton.

In 2026 the Brewers won the National League pennant by sweeping the Los Angeles Dodgers four games to none, with Jake Bauers named NLCS MVP. Now they face Tampa Bay for the first championship in team history.

An ode to Bob

For fifty-four seasons, summer in Milwaukee sounded like Bob Uecker. "Mr. Baseball" called Brewers games on the radio from 1971 through 2024, and every night with him felt like company.

  1. 1956A Milwaukee kid signs with his hometown Braves.
  2. 1962Starts six seasons as a big-league backup catcher with the Braves, Cardinals and Phillies. He liked to say a career highlight was an intentional walk from Sandy Koufax.
  3. 1964Wins a World Series ring with the St. Louis Cardinals.
  4. 1971Joins the Brewers radio booth, where he stays for the rest of his life.
  5. 1980sJohnny Carson calls him "Mr. Baseball." He stars on Mr. Belvedere, sells Miller Lite and plays announcer Harry Doyle in Major League.
  6. 2003Receives the Ford C. Frick Award from the Baseball Hall of Fame.
  7. 2025Dies on January 16 at age 90. Fans still sit beside his bronze statue in the Uecker Seat.

He never got to call a Brewers championship. This little booth keeps a light on for him.
