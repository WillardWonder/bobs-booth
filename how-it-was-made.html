How Bob was made

Bob is an AI radio announcer who calls Brewers World Series games live, pitch by pitch, for free. Here's how he works, in plain language, and the free tools behind him.

What happens on a game night

  1. Bob's studio wakes up. About half an hour before first pitch, a free online timer tells GitHub to start Bob's studio on a free cloud computer with two graphics cards. Nobody has to press a button.
  2. Bob watches the game. Every couple of seconds the studio reads MLB's public game data: every pitch, its speed, the count, every hit and how hard it was hit, the score.
  3. AI writes Bob's next line. An AI writer turns each pitch or play into a radio call in Bob's voice. Quick, routine lines come from a small AI model running right on the studio computer; bigger moments go to Google's Gemini.
  4. Every line is fact-checked first. Before Bob says anything, the studio checks it against the real data: the count, the outs, any number, the series score. If something's wrong or repeats an earlier joke, the line is rewritten.
  5. An AI voice speaks it. An open-source text-to-speech model turns the line into Bob's voice, with a calm voice for routine plays and an excited one for big moments.
  6. Everyone hears the same broadcast. Each line is placed on a shared timeline about 15 seconds behind the field, so every listener hears the same moment, like real radio.
  7. The replay saves itself. When the game ends, the timer copies the finished broadcast here as a replay you can play anytime, skip around, share, or download.

Keeping Bob honest

AI writers are great at turning real data into lively radio and bad at remembering facts on their own. So Bob only talks about what he's given: MLB's live data, real postseason numbers, the series so far, and a checked list of Brewers history. Lines with a number that isn't in the data, a wrong count, a false series score, or a joke he already told get rewritten before they air.

About Bob

Bob is an AI announcer made for fun, in the spirit of Brewers radio and as a tribute to the late Bob Uecker. Bob is not Bob Uecker, and nothing Bob says was said by Uecker. This fan page is not connected to MLB or the Milwaukee Brewers.
